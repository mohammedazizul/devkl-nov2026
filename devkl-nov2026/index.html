How is Mizan certified halal?

Audited annually by an SC Malaysia-registered Shariah adviser using strict AAOIFI screening.

Is my money safe if Mizan closes?

Yes. Assets sit in an independent licensed trustee custodian account under your own name.

Great alongside them! Use TH/ASB for core local savings, and Mizan for flexible goal pots & global halal equities.
