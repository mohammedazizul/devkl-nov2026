4 honest answers before you decide

The exact questions first-time visitors usually ask before trusting a financial platform.

Who certifies that Mizan is truly Shariah-compliant?

Every portfolio is reviewed and audited annually by an independent Shariah Advisory panel registered with Securities Commission Malaysia standards, following AAOIFI screening rules. You can download the Shariah certificate for every fund right inside the app.

What happens to my money if Mizan shuts down?

Mizan never holds your money in our company bank account. Your cash and fund units are held in a segregated custodian trust account under your name with a licensed trustee — meaning your assets remain legally yours even if Mizan ceased operations.

Is my money locked in, or are there withdrawal penalties?

Zero lock-in and RM 0 withdrawal fee. Cash Shield withdrawals arrive in your Malaysian bank account within 1 business day, and investment portfolio withdrawals settle within 3–4 business days.

Do I need to be Muslim to use Mizan?

Not at all! Over a quarter of ethical investors choose Shariah-screened portfolios simply because they avoid high-debt companies, weapons, gambling, and tobacco. You can turn Zakat tracking off with one click.
